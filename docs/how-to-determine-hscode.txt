 1. Establish the product facts:
      - Material/composition
      - Primary function
      - Processing state
      - Physical form
      - Intended use
      - How it operates
      - Whether it is a complete product, part, mixture, or set

  2. Apply GRI/KUM:
      - GRI 1: heading wording and applicable Section/Chapter Notes
      - GRI 2: incomplete goods, mixtures, and combinations
      - GRI 3: specificity, essential character, then numerical order
      - GRI 4: most similar goods
      - GRI 5: cases and packaging
      - GRI 6: comparison between subheadings at the same level

  3. Navigate the hierarchy:

  Section
  → Chapter
  → Heading (4 digits)
  → HS subheading (6 digits)
  → AHTN/BTKI tariff post (8 digits)

  4. Check exclusions and redirects in Section and Chapter Notes.
  5. Compare only headings or subheadings at the same hierarchical level.
  6. Produce alternatives and explain why they were rejected.
  7. Validate the final eight-digit code against the BTKI edition effective on the declaration date.
